# Math lesson design guide (anonymous)

This public repository contains reusable lesson templates and general teaching guidelines. Do not commit learner names, ages, family details, assessment scores, observations, dates of participation, or exported lesson reports.

## Design principles
- Language: Ukrainian. Friendly, adventurous, not babyish.
- Sessions: approximately 25–30 minutes; 10–12 meaningful tasks.
- Mix representations: number lines, balancing, number machines, objects, shops, mysteries, short stories and two-step problems.
- Build number composition, comparisons by difference, interpreting short mathematical stories, and reasoning.
- Put challenging work earlier and finish with an accessible success.
- An adult may record independence (3 independent, 2 uncertain, 1 with help, 0 not yet), private notes, and review flags.
- Fatigue can affect performance: do not interpret late-lesson difficulty as proof of a skill gap.
- Reports remain in local browser storage and are exported manually. Never commit individual reports or personal data.
- For future lessons, ask for privately shared feedback rather than storing learner-specific information in this public repository.
