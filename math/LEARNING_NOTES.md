# Kiddo — Ivan's mathematics learning record

Updated: 2026-11-11. This repository is PUBLIC. Keep this record educational and avoid private or sensitive information.

## Learner and approach
- Ivan is five years old, reads confidently, recognizes numbers, and enjoys dinosaurs, LEGO, superheroes, mysteries and game-like challenges.
- Ukrainian is the lesson language. Avoid babyish visuals, repetitive worksheets and unreasonably long sessions.
- Adult facilitates and records independence (3 = independent, 2 = slow/uncertain, 1 = hint, 0 = not yet), notes, and revisit flags.
- Keep lessons ~25–30 minutes, 10–12 substantial tasks, with the most cognitively demanding work before fatigue. Stop early when concentration fades. End with a pleasant, achievable win.
- Physical objects (LEGO, coins, tokens) may support reasoning. Use a variety of interfaces, representations and story contexts rather than repeating the same problem format.
- Results are saved in browser localStorage; reports are exported manually, NOT sent to GitHub.
